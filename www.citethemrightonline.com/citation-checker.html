      CTR Reference Checker

      The CTR Reference Checker scans your essay for in-text citations and compares them with your reference list. It helps identify:

      • ✅ Matched citations — correctly listed in the reference list.
      • ❌ Missing references — cited in text but not found in references.
      • ⚠️ Uncited references — appear in the list but not cited in the essay.

      How it works

      Paste your essay and reference list separately. The tool highlights citations directly in your essay — green for correct, red for missing, and yellow for uncertain matches.

      Under the hood

      CTR uses pattern recognition (regular expressions) to detect author–date style citations and match them to your reference list. It performs structural checks only — not formatting or order validation.
